When are these estimates reliable?
  • The model was validated in trained male cyclists during a sustained, evenly paced, individual uphill time trial (9.2 km, ~7% average gradient, ~30 min, low wind).
  • In that study, estimated PO showed a trivial bias, with individual differences of about ±6.5% (95% limits of agreement).
  • Accuracy is expected to decrease on shallower climbs, in windy conditions, when riding in a group (drafting), with variable pacing, or in short efforts.
